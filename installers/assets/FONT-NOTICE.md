# Installer typeface

Voice Sans is a limited UI-character subset of Noto Sans SC, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 in `OFL.txt`. The derivative family is renamed Voice Sans. Regular and SemiBold are static instances of weights 400 and 600. The subset is bundled solely for consistent installer typography, not installed into the operating system.

Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
