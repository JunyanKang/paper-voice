It has been shown for the retina of nonprimate mammals (Franze et al., 2007; Agte et al., 2011; Labin et al., 2014), and proposed for the human and avian retina (Labin et al., 2014; Zueva et al., 2014), that Müller cells act as living optical fibers which guide the light with minimal intensity loss through the inner retinal layers toward the photoreceptors.
